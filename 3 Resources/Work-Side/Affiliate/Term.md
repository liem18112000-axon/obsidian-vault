## Pricing models — how you get paid

| Term | One-liner |
|------|-----------|
| [[Cost per Action]] (CPA) | Umbrella: paid only when a referred visitor completes a defined action. |
| [[Cost per Lead]] (CPL) | Paid when the visitor becomes a lead (form/sign-up) — no purchase. |
| [[Cost per Quality Lead]] (CPQL) | CPL with a quality gate: only leads that pass criteria pay. |
| [[Cost per Registration]] (CPR) | Paid when the visitor creates a working account. |
| [[Cost per Sale]] (CPS) | Paid only on an actual purchase, usually a % of order value. |
| [[Revenue Share]] (RevShare) | Recurring % of a referred customer's ongoing revenue. |

## Tracking & rules — what gates the payout

| Term | One-liner |
|------|-----------|
| [[Cookie Duration]] | How long after the click an action still earns you commission. |
| [[Attribution Model]] | Which affiliate gets credited when several touched the sale (usually last-click). |
| [[Reversal]] | Clawback of commission when an action turns out invalid (refund, junk lead). |

## Metrics — how you compare offers

| Term | One-liner |
|------|-----------|
| [[Earnings per Click]] (EPC) | Average commission per click — the best single number for comparing offers. |
| [[Average Order Value]] (AOV) | Average spend per order; the multiplier on %-based commission. |
| [[Lifetime Value]] (LTV) | Total revenue a customer generates over their whole lifetime; decides RevShare vs one-off. |

## Reading order for a newcomer

1. [[Cost per Action]] — the family the payout models belong to.
2. [[Cost per Sale]] and [[Cost per Lead]] — the two everyday models.
3. [[Earnings per Click]] — how to compare any two offers honestly.
4. [[Cookie Duration]] + [[Attribution Model]] — why you do/don't get credited.
5. [[Reversal]] — why confirmed earnings are lower than pending.
6. [[Revenue Share]] + [[Lifetime Value]] — the recurring model and the maths behind it.
