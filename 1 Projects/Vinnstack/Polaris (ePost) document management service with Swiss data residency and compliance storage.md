# Polaris (ePost): document management service with Swiss data residency and compliance storage

Polaris is an enterprise digital document management service from ePost (Swiss PostalService AG). It offers document intake automation, compliance-managed storage with Swiss data residency, multi-channel delivery (email, SMS, physical mail), and team collaboration via encrypted Communities. Protected by Google IAP suggests it is a B2B service. Integration opportunities for Vinnstack: (1) document intake—pull business documents from Polaris inbox for agent analysis; (2) compliance storage—write generated content to Polaris with retention policies; (3) workflow automation—trigger multi-channel delivery of agent outputs; (4) archival—long-term compliant storage of interrogations, PRDs, skills. Swiss data residency is a differentiator for EU-regulated workloads.
